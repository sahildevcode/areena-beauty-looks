# Areena Beauty Looks ♡ — Bridal Studio & Salon

> Luxury bridal makeover studio, trendsetting salon treatments, and viral beauty essentials. Winner of Business Conclave Excellence Awards 2024.

## ✨ Features
- **Aesthetic Preloader Animation**: Real-time 0–100% progress counter with soft glowing blush-pink theme.
- **Micro-Interactions & Hover Effects**: 3D card tilts, image zooms, and floating sticker animations.
- **Celebrity Bridal & Glam Portfolio**: Real client bridal makeover transformations with full-screen zoom preview.
- **Award-Winning Excellence**: Felicitated at Business Conclave & Excellence Awards 2024 by celebrity guest Sneha Ullal.
- **Permanent Fixed Quick-Connect Bar**: Direct right-side floating action links to Instagram (`@llll_rakhi_llll`) and Google Maps Studio Location.
- **Interactive E-Commerce Suite**: Slide-in Cart Drawer, Wishlist pop animations, Quick View Modal, and live search.
